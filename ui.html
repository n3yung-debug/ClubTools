Import squad
On FUT.GG: link your EA account (Club → Import), load your squad into the Squad Builder, press Share, and paste the link here. Formation and manager come across too.
FUTBIN blocks programs from reading its pages, so this works through your browser instead:
  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: → ClubTools — or , then in your browser add a new bookmark, name it “→ ClubTools” and paste into its URL field.
  2. On FUTBIN, open your squad (after My Club → Import my club, build or open the squad you play).
  3. With ClubTools running, click the bookmark. This app opens with the squad ready to check.
The same bookmark works on a FUT.GG squad page. FUTBIN's page layout couldn't be tested from here, so if the bookmark says it found no players, tell me and I'll adjust it.
